Project Overview

Built on Collaboration

BAJMC handled the visual language — photography, editing, lighting, framing & thematic concepts — while FD led the creative direction on the ground: models, outfits, makeup, hair & styling.

Photography & Editing

BAJMC curated shot lists, controlled lighting setups and executed post-production editing for a cohesive visual tone.

Model & Styling

FD sourced outfits, managed makeup/hair design and directed model poses to embody each thematic concept.

Thematic Concepts

Joint brainstorming produced a narrative arc across the lookbook — each spread tells a connected story.

Exhibition & Print

Files are prepared in both digital (web/scroll) and print-ready CMYK formats for display or placement portfolios.
