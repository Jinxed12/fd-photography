Project Overview

Built on Collaboration

This collaborative lookbook brings the whole creative process together. BAJMC shaped the visual language through photography, lighting, composition and post-production; FD led the on-set direction through model casting, outfits, makeup, hair and styling. Each story is prepared for both digital viewing and exhibition or placement use.

Photography & Editing

BAJMC plans the shot list, selects camera and lens setups, and shapes lighting, framing and composition. The photographs are then color graded and edited into a consistent visual collection.

Model & Styling

FD coordinates talent and outfits, develops makeup and hair, and directs poses and on-set styling so every look feels intentional and supports the story.

Thematic Concepts

Both teams develop a shared visual direction before the shoot. Each lookbook collection follows a connected mood, helping the images work together as an editorial narrative.

Exhibition & Print

The final work is organized for a responsive online lookbook, screen and projector presentations, and print-ready portfolio or exhibition layouts.
